# Pondera Team 13 Code of Conduct

## Decision Making

Decisions will be made by consensus, in the event of disagreement, a compromise will be found. 
In the event of a tie where absolutely no compromise can be found, we'll flip a coin.

## Attendance

Absences can be fine with a three day notice that a member of the group won't be showing up with a valid excuse.
In the event that a member misses a meeting with no excuse, we'll catch them up remotely and attribute work accordingly.

## Assignments

Assignments will be taken by volunteering, and in the event that a member does a mediocre job on their assignment,
the team will reconvene and allow for a correction after feedback has been provided.

## Participation

As assignments are done by volunteering, members will be allocated equal work if the balance becomes tilted. We'll honor members
strength and weaknesses by talking about contentious assignments and ensuring everyone is comfortable with the result.

## Meeting times & Location

The team will find meeting times & locations arbitrarily via the group chat, as we have volatile schedules and meet three times
a week naturally in class.

## Promptness

We expect that everything be done within the deadline that it's assigned, and if things are beginning to run late we'll
get together, review why it was late and what went wrong, and decide based on workload as to what to do further.

## Conversational Courtesies

We'll put our phones away for matters that aren't related to the project, and engage in our work and elaborating on our segments
even if they're solo ventures. This will allow for strong teambuilding, engaging others strengths, and keeping eachother
up to date with development.

## Enforcement & Feedback

The team will update eachother on their progress every meeting and hold eachother accountable to their assignment due dates by the end of the meetings. We'll give feedback individually on each major assignment, and then give independent feedback so that there's unbiased points of view on everyone's workflow.
